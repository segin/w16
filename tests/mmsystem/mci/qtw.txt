# QuickTime for Windows' MCI driver MCIQTW.DRV (behind MDSC3) on the
# QuickTime-installed drive (tests/mmsystem/mcirun.sh qt): its sample
# movie opened by its extension ([mci extensions] mov=QTWVideo), queried,
# played to the end with wait, and closed; then with notify
s sysinfo all quantity
s sysinfo all name 4
s open c:\windows\sample.mov alias m
s capability m device type
s capability m can play
s capability m has video
s info m product
s status m mode
s status m length
s status m time format
s status m media present
s set m time format ms
s status m length
s where m destination
s play m wait
s status m mode
s status m position
s seek m to start
s play m notify
w 15000
s status m mode
s close m
s open qtwvideo alias q
s close q
